Cyber Safety

Follow these simple practices to stay safe online

🔐 Protect Your Password

Use strong and unique passwords for your accounts. Never share passwords, PINs or verification codes with anyone.

🎣 Stay Safe From Phishing

Avoid suspicious links, emails and messages. Verify the sender before providing personal or financial information.

💳 Protect Online Payments

Verify payment requests carefully. Never share OTPs, UPI PINs, card PINs or banking passwords with unknown persons.

📱 Secure Your Devices

Keep your operating system, applications and security software updated. Install applications only from trusted sources.

🌐 Be Careful Online

Avoid unknown websites and downloads. Check website addresses carefully before entering sensitive information.

🚨 Report Suspicious Activity

If you notice a suspicious cyber incident, preserve the available evidence and report the incident through the reporting system.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I report a cyber threat?

Create an account, log in and use the Report Cyber Threat option to submit your incident.

Can I track my report?

Yes. After logging in, you can use the Track Complaint service to check your submitted report and current status.

Who manages the reports?

Administrators can review reports and update their status through the administration dashboard.
